Indian Institute of technology

Established in 1959 and recognised as an Institute of national Importance, the National Institute of Technology Warangal (NITW) is a premier Technical and Research University. More than a decade back, the Institute embarked on realigning the campus to meet specific goals for its long term growth, including creation of an environment that promotes interaction and learning. As empanelled Consultants for Architecture and Interiors, Collage Design worked closely with the various NITW departments to deliver State-of-the-Art facilities for the students and staff.
